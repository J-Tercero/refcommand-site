02

Sideline Management & Control

The sole weekly study emphasis · NFHS 2026 Point of Emphasis

Restricted area / team box

Dead Ball: Three Coaches Maximum

The restricted area is immediately outside the sideline and extends at least 2 yards out of bounds in front of the team box. The team box is outside the restricted area and normally extends between the 25-yard lines.

THREE COACHES — AND ONLY COACHESmay occupy the restricted area while the ball is dead between plays, including to signal offensive or defensive plays.

As the ball is about to become live, those coaches must retreat completely into the team box. Other substitutes, trainers and team personnel remain in the team box unless otherwise permitted by rule.

No coach, substitute, trainer or other nonplayer may occupy the restricted area while the ball is live.

Separate foul · contact with an official

Contact Is Not the Next Step

If an official unintentionally contacts a coach or other nonplayer illegally occupying the restricted area while the ball is live:

15-yard nonplayer illegal personal contact foulEnforce from the succeeding spot.

The crew does not need to first progress through the warning and 5-yard sideline penalties. A second such offense results in disqualification of the head coach.

Coaches addressing officials

Keep Discussions in the Team Area

Coaches should remain in their designated team area when discussing rulings. A coach may not enter the field simply to dispute or challenge a ruling. The 2026 Point of Emphasis calls for enforcement when coaches or other team personnel enter the field to dispute rulings or address officials outside the coaching area.

Coaches should not approach officials with phones, tablets, video or other electronic devices in an attempt to show or review a play.

Rule 2-6-1

Legitimate Coach-Referee Conference

For a possible rule misapplication, the referee confers with the coach at the sideline in front of the coach’s team box in the field of play. This is different from a coach entering the field to argue or dispute a judgment call.

04

2026 Operational Takeaway

NFHS Point of Emphasis

Clear. Communicate. Enforce.

  • Keep the restricted area clear.
  • Keep nonplayers in the team box when required.
  • Three coaches maximum in the restricted area while the ball is dead.
  • Clear the restricted area before the ball becomes live.
  • Address violations when observed rather than allowing the sideline to progressively deteriorate.
  • Coaches remain in the team area for discussions with officials.
  • Do not allow coaches or team personnel to enter the field to dispute rulings.
  • Do not allow electronic or video devices to be brought to officials to challenge a ruling.
  • Maintain professional communication while enforcing the rule consistently.
